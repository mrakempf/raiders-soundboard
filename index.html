Raiders Basketball Soundboard

Tap a pad to play it, tap it again (or press Esc) to stop. Star a pad to pin it under Favorites. Sound pads play over the practice music, and the music dips while they do.

The practice player keeps going on its own: when a song ends the next one starts. Space plays or pauses it, and phone lock-screen controls work too.
